# I Sold My AI Startup Before Revenue: Here’s What Investors Missed — And Founders Shouldn’t

Markdown Content:
_By [Alexander Kardos-Nyheim](https://www.crunchbase.com/person/alexander-kardos-nyheim-d1ec)_ ^[raw/articles/i-sold-my-ai-startup-before-revenue-here-s-what-investors-missed-and-founders-sh.md]

I sold my AI research company while I was qualifying as a lawyer in the U.K. I built [Safe Sign Technologies](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/safe-sign-technologies) with researchers from [Cambridge](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/university-of-cambridge), [DeepMind](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/deepmind), [Harvard](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/harvard-university) and [MIT](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/massachusetts-institute-of-technology-mit) who believed in the mission enough to trust a 21-year-old law student to lead the ship. ^[raw/articles/i-sold-my-ai-startup-before-revenue-here-s-what-investors-missed-and-founders-sh.md]

[Roughly 20 months later](https://www.crunchbase.com/acquisition/thomson-reuters-acquires-safe-sign-technologies--efce0c25), when [Thomson Reuters](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/thomson-reuters) acquired us, it was the first time in its 170-year history it bought a company pre-revenue. Thomson Reuters acquired us for the science. ^[raw/articles/i-sold-my-ai-startup-before-revenue-here-s-what-investors-missed-and-founders-sh.md]

![Image 1: Alexander Kardos-Nyheim, angel investor, Thomson Reuters Labs](https://news.crunchbase.com/wp-content/uploads/Alexander-Kardos-Nyheim_headshot-e1781617252324-254x300.jpg)

Getting there was painful, though. Our published papers put the model among the best in the world at legal reasoning, and we trained it for a fraction of what the large labs were spending. We had been a quieter version of “the [DeepSeek](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/deepseek) story,” developing very capable models using novel algorithms with huge capital efficiency. ^[raw/articles/i-sold-my-ai-startup-before-revenue-here-s-what-investors-missed-and-founders-sh.md]

None of that counted for much in the rooms I walked into. Investors always asked about the product and the traction. U.K. investors passed, and I ended up raising most of our funding in the United States. ^[raw/articles/i-sold-my-ai-startup-before-revenue-here-s-what-investors-missed-and-founders-sh.md]

I back founders now, and the things I weigh have stayed consistent. As a founder, I was told again and again that science meant little until it was bolted onto a product. That test was wrong then and I believe it is fatal now. ^[raw/articles/i-sold-my-ai-startup-before-revenue-here-s-what-investors-missed-and-founders-sh.md]

In the first quarter of 2026, foundational AI startups raised around [$178 billion](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/foundational-ai-startup-funding-doubled-openai-anthropic-xai-q1-2026/). The market is realizing that foundational AI is where the long-term value sits, and this is the year we may see the exits and IPOs that prove the bet right. ^[raw/articles/i-sold-my-ai-startup-before-revenue-here-s-what-investors-missed-and-founders-sh.md]
