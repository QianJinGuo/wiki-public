1. Model cards describe how a model works, its intended use and license, training data, performance, and limitations. ^[raw/articles/how-to-automate-ai-model-documentation-with-the-nvidia-mcg-t-806efb.md]
2. They promote transparency and accountability so downstream users—customers, regulators, and affected communities—can make informed decisions when selecting and deploying AI. ^[raw/articles/how-to-automate-ai-model-documentation-with-the-nvidia-mcg-t-806efb.md]
3. That audience extends beyond developers: Policymakers, procurement teams, and risk assessors rely on model cards to evaluate fitness for use and compare models across vendors. ^[raw/articles/how-to-automate-ai-model-documentation-with-the-nvidia-mcg-t-806efb.md]
4. In practice, creating model cards manually is tedious and slow. ^[raw/articles/how-to-automate-ai-model-documentation-with-the-nvidia-mcg-t-806efb.md]
